# How to apply at Instant Mechanic

You can do everything on your phone.

## 1. Make an account

Open the website and press **Create account**. Write your full name, your email and a password
(at least 12 letters or numbers). Then press **Sign in**.

## 2. Upload your resume

Go to **My resume**. Choose your resume (PDF or Word file, up to 8 MB), tick the box to agree, and press
**Upload resume**. You only do this once. You need a resume before you can apply.

No resume file? Make a simple one in Word or Google Docs: your name, phone, city, the work you have done,
the vehicles you can repair, and your training. Save it as PDF.

## 3. Apply

Go to **Jobs**, read the job, and press **Apply**. That's all.

## 4. Follow your application

**My applications** shows where you are:

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| Applied | We have received your application |
| Screening | Our team is reading your application and resume |
| Shortlisted | You are on the shortlist. We will contact you soon |
| Trade test | A short practical test of your skills |
| Interview | A conversation with our team |
| Offer | We would like to offer you the job |
| Hired | You have joined Instant Mechanic |

We also send you an email at every step. Check your spam folder too.

## 5. Interviews

Under **Interviews** you see the date, time and place or video link. Press **I will attend** or
**I cannot attend** so we know.

## Your data

Your resume is seen only by our hiring team. You can delete your resume, or ask us to delete all your
data, from **My resume > Your data**.

Forgot your password? Press **Forgot password** on the sign-in page.
