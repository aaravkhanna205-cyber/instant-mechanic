# HR manager guide (Neeraj)

You have **full access** to Instant Mechanic's hiring: jobs, candidates, interviews, decisions, the team,
every candidate email, reports and exports. Sign in at the site address with your own email and password.

## Your daily 10 minutes

1. **Dashboard** - new applications, upcoming interviews, open vacancies.
2. **Notifications** (bell, top right) - new applications, interview replies, deletion requests.
3. **Notifications > Emails to candidates** - every email the system sent or tried to send. Filter **Failed** to see problems.
4. **Candidates** - open new people, read their resume evidence, move them to the next stage with a short note.

## Jobs

- **Jobs > New vacancy**: title, category, location, description, requirements. Save as **Draft** while writing.
- Change the status to **Published** to show it to candidates. **Paused** hides it for a while, **Closed** ends it.
- **Agent workflows > Job description** can draft a description from your notes. You edit and approve it.

## Moving candidates through the stages

Applied → Screening → Shortlisted → Trade test → Interview → Offer → Hired (or Rejected / Withdrawn).

- On a candidate's page pick the new stage under **Move to stage**, add a note (only staff see notes) and press **Move**.
- The candidate gets an email and a portal update in simple words. Nothing moves by itself.
- **Show stage history** lists who moved what and when.

## Interviews

- On the candidate page: **Date and time**, optional **Meeting link**, then **Schedule interview**.
- The candidate is emailed and can answer **I will attend** or **I cannot attend**; you get a notification.
- After the interview, open it under **Interviews** and record the status and feedback.

## Candidate emails

- Candidates are emailed when they apply, when their stage changes and when an interview is scheduled, moved or cancelled.
- Status meanings: **Waiting to send** (normally under a minute), **Sent** (Gmail accepted it), **Stored (outbox mode)** (real email is off), **Failed** (see the error column), **Skipped** (no email address).
- Switching real email on is a one-time step on the server PC: [GMAIL-SETUP.md](GMAIL-SETUP.md).

## Reports and exports

- **Reports**: hiring funnel, conversion between stages, numbers by job, team activity.
- **Find applications**: search by name, job, stage or date, then **Download these applications** (CSV for Excel).
- Exports contain personal data. Keep them on the company PC and delete them when finished.

## Team and roles

- **Team and roles > Add someone**: the person registers first, then you add their email with a role.
- Roles: HR manager and Hiring manager (full access), Recruiter (no team management), Interviewer (assigned interviews only).
- The last full-access person cannot be removed, so the team can never lock itself out.

## Candidate data and privacy

- Candidates can ask to delete their data from their portal. The Super Admin approves it under **Super Admin > Deletion requests**.
- Keep notes factual and about the job. Never write about religion, caste, health, family plans or similar.
- The AI only suggests. A person makes every decision; write the reason in the stage note.
