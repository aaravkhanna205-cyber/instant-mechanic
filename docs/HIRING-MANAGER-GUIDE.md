# Hiring manager guide (Aarav)

You have **full access**: you shortlist, interview and decide. Everything you do is recorded with your name.

## A simple hiring routine

1. **Candidates** - sort new applications. Open each one: profile, resume evidence (sentences from the resume that match the job's requirements), stage history.
2. **Shortlist** - move good matches to **Shortlisted** with a one-line reason ("3 years two-wheeler repair, lives in Noida").
3. **Trade test** (for mechanics) - move to **Trade test**, run the practical check, write the result in the note.
4. **Interview** - schedule it on the candidate page. Use **Interview Studio** for an approved question set; **Agent workflows > Interview summary** drafts a summary you check.
5. **Decide** - **Offer**, then **Hired**, or **Rejected**. The candidate is told politely in simple words.

## Tips for fair decisions

- Judge every candidate for a job by the same requirements. Write them in the job before reading resumes.
- The resume evidence shows where a skill is mentioned, not whether it is true. Check in the trade test or interview.
- Give a reason for every rejection in the note; it protects the company if a decision is questioned.
- Reports > **Conversion** shows where candidates drop out. A long wait in one stage means candidates are waiting for us.

## What the Super Admin does (you or whoever runs the PC)

- **Super Admin > People and roles**: make someone Super Admin, change any role.
- **System health**: database, emails waiting or failed, resumes failed, **Server errors** (any repeating error should be fixed).
- **Data retention**: remove old closed candidates after the period you choose.
